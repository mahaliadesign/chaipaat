Our Story

Born in Assam. Crafted with Care. Shared with the World

Chaipaat was born with a simple yet meaningful vision — to serve the world the real taste of Assam tea.

For us, tea is more than a beverage. It is an emotion, a tradition, and a symbol of purity that brings people together. Assam, with its lush green tea gardens and generations of tea-growing heritage, is renowned worldwide for its bold, rich and distinctive tea. Its deep colour, strong character and captivating aroma make Assam tea truly special.

Chaipaat represents that spirit of Assam.

Our customers are at the heart of everything we do. The trust they place in us is reflected in the fact that 99% of our customers come back to us again — a relationship we value far beyond a simple purchase.

Our customers can communicate directly with us, share their preferences, ask questions and consult with our team. We listen, understand and continuously learn from the people who enjoy our tea.

Chaipaat brings together two thoughtfully created experiences — Authentic Tea, for those who cherish the traditional taste of Assam, and Customised Tea, thoughtfully developed for people who want to make tea a part of their health-conscious lifestyle.

At Chaipaat, we are committed to bringing together taste, authenticity, wellness and care — while staying rooted in the land that inspires us.

Founder: Diti Deka

Founders Bite

“Growing up surrounded by the lush tea estates of Assam, tea was never just a morning ritual—it was a part of our life. The earthy aroma of freshly plucked leaves and the misty mornings remain deeply rooted in our memories.

We founded Chaipaat to bring that authentic Assam experience to your cup. Rooted in fertile soils and inspired by sustainable practices, our teas are crafted to preserve the natural character and purity of every leaf.

From bold, malty ginger CTC blends to delicate whole-leaf green teas, every pack carries the rich aroma, warmth, and spirit of Assam—from our home to your cup.”
